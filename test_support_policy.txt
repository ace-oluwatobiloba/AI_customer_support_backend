AceXcell Customer Support Test Policy

Account Access:
Customers who cannot access their accounts should first verify that they are using the correct registered email address.

Password Reset:
Customers who forget their passwords should use the password reset feature. They should never share their passwords or verification codes with support agents.

Escalation:
If a customer cannot resolve an account access problem after following the documented steps, the support system should create or update a support ticket for review by a human support agent.

Business Hours:
Support requests should be recorded even when human agents are unavailable. The system should retain the conversation and allow the support team to follow up.
